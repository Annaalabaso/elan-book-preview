The big idea

Small, practical tools. Meaningful leadership impact.

Everyday leadership involves choices. The goal of this page is to introduce the book’s practical direction without pretending its chapters or publication details have been finalized.

01

Know which role to take

A situation may call for listening, coaching, teaching, or advising. Effective leaders pay attention before choosing their approach.

02

Communicate with intention

Build your awareness of how you listen, communicate, and bring people together.

03

Practice accountability

Support people and teams with thoughtful delegation, clear expectations, and learning opportunities.
